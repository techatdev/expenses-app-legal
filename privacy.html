Privacy policy

This app keeps your financial data on your own device. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose to export or share it.

Where your data lives

Categories, transactions, borrowers, loans and settings are stored in a local database on your device. There is no account and no server holding a copy.

What we collect

Nothing automatically. The app has no analytics or advertising SDK and does not send your entries to us. The contact picker is only opened when you choose it, and it does not upload your contacts.

Personal information you enter

Borrower names, phone numbers, addresses, lender names and notes are sensitive. Enter only what you need and use your device lock. A backup file contains this information, so keep it somewhere safe.

Backups

Backups are opt-in. Settings → Backup & restore can export a file and import it again. The file stays on your device or in whatever location you choose when you share it; it is never sent to us automatically.

Cloud sync

Cloud sync is not enabled. Your entries are not uploaded to any server. If a cloud option is added later it will be opt-in, and this policy will be updated before it starts.

Support

If you email techatdev@gmail.com, we only receive what you choose to include. Please do not send your PIN or recovery code. Include the app version and a description of the problem instead.

Deleting data

Removing the app removes its local database. Individual transactions can be deleted inside the app; deleted transactions are kept recoverable until the app is removed. Export a backup first if you want to keep them.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the app grows. The date at the bottom of this page shows the last revision.
